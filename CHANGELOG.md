# Changelog

## buwan 1.10.0 · buwan-premiere 1.7.0 — 2026-11-09  (/plugin marketplace update buwan-films, update both plugins, restart)
**The basics no longer grade and no longer touch speech.** `/buwan:basics` and `/buwan-premiere:wedding-day` now do two things into `<raw>_ENHANCED_<date-time>/`: the exposure fix on every clip and the best cut + smooth glide on shaky Creative/Glider clips. Every other clip, interviews and vows included, keeps its original audio and full length.
- **No test render by default:** the basics go straight to the full render (shaky clips first, spot-check one while the rest renders). A short `--limit 2` test only after an engine update or with a new camera.
- **No grade in the basics.** `render` bakes no look by default (`--look <.cube>` only when asked). The exposure fix now runs as a 16-bit RGB gain: the same result within 2 luma points, and the whole render is about 2.4× faster than exposure + look (8.6 s → 3.6 s per 10 s of 1080p60 10-bit).
- **`/buwan:grade` applies the look, on request.** It builds the look from the studio's film (`assemble.js look --reference`, before/after sheet) or uses the stock LUTs, and applies it in the editing app. Premiere: the editor picks it once in Lumetri and the bridge copies it (`grade {look_from}`).
- **`/buwan:multicam` is now interviews, vows and speeches** (new `assemble.js speech`). The editor lists a moment's clips (cameras + lav/recorder), and the engine:
  - picks the best audio (SNR, clipping, level; a sound-only file wins a tie);
  - syncs the other angles to it by sound (2.5 ms; quiet camera mics still sync);
  - cleans it (high-pass, noise reduction, voice EQ);
  - cuts the dead air at the same points on every angle (20 ms fades, words never cut);
  - renders each angle with its exposure fixed, no grade, HEVC 10-bit, the clean audio at −17 LUFS / peaks −6.
  - Output: `<raw>_SPEECH_<date-time>/INTERVIEWS|VOWS|SPEECHES/<moment>/`. Each angle holds only what it covered and starts at its timecode in the cut moment, so Premiere / Final Cut / Resolve build the multicam **by timecode**. A clip that doesn't match by sound is left out and listed. Moments are saved in `edit/assemble/speech_moments.json`.
- **Camera picture profile in the house rules:** S-Cinetone 10-bit 4:2:2 on 10-bit bodies; Cine4 + Pro color on 8-bit bodies (never S-Log); drone Normal on SDE days; white balance in Kelvin. No S-Log3 on same-day jobs (the engine has no Log→709 step).
- Skills (basics, wedding-day, multicam, grade), house rules, session card, README, MAINTAINING and the pipeline pages were updated.

## buwan 1.9.0 · buwan-premiere 1.6.0 — 2026-11-09  (/plugin marketplace update buwan-films, update both plugins, restart; then /buwan:setup-kit once to build the vid.stab ffmpeg)
**The basics are now enhanced clips, nothing else.** `/buwan:basics` and `/buwan-premiere:wedding-day` read the raw folder (never written) and render new clips into a new folder next to it on every run, `<raw>_ENHANCED_<date-time>/`, with the same camera/card folders and names. No copying, no dedupe, no file organizing, no Premiere bins in the basics (those skills stay, on request).
- **`assemble.js render`:** every camera clip gets the exposure fix and then the studio look, encoded as HEVC 10-bit 4:2:0, 80 Mbps, Rec.709 tags (VideoToolbox).
  - Speech clips (interviews, letters, speeches, vows) are cut to their speech ranges with 20 ms fades, laid on the cleaned audio, and levelled again after the cut: −17 LUFS, peaks −6 dBFS. Tested at −17.8 / −16.9 LUFS on clips that came out of the clean-up at −21.3 / −18.2.
  - A speech clip with silent camera audio renders whole and is flagged.
  - Shaky Creative/Glider clips are cut to their best part and stabilized with vid.stab (two passes, smoothing 25, optimal zoom).
  - Other clips keep their original audio.
  - Speech and shaky clips render first. The run is resumable (`--out`), test runs use `--only speech|shaky|clip --limit N`, and every clip is logged in `render_log.csv`.
- **`assemble.js look --reference <studio film>`:** builds the studio look from the studio's own film.
  - The reference frames have the letterbox cropped, and black, title and B&W frames are skipped. This wedding's footage is sampled with its exposure fix applied.
  - Matched in CIE Lab: the tone curve takes 70% of the reference's contrast, with a soft film shoulder (L* 100 → 95, so whites stay white).
  - Color: a/b mean and spread are matched in 5 lightness bands, with saturation capped at +10% and near-whites protected.
  - Output: a 33-point `.cube`, `edit/assemble/look.json`, and a before / after preview sheet.
- `scripts/build-ffmpeg-vidstab.sh` builds ffmpeg 7.1.1 with libvidstab + VideoToolbox into `~/.buwan/ffmpeg-vidstab` (the system ffmpeg is untouched). `/buwan:setup-kit` runs it on macOS.
- Skills, house rules, session card, README, MAINTAINING and the pipeline pages describe the new basics. `bycategory`, `relink`, Premiere sub-bins and the `basics` bridge step remain available on request.

## buwan 1.8.0 · buwan-premiere 1.5.0 — 2026-11-09  (/plugin marketplace update buwan-films, then update both plugins and restart; reload the MCP Bridge panel)
- **The working copy, organized by category** (`assemble.js bycategory`): `<raw>_WORK/BY_CATEGORY/<category>[/Bride prep · Groom prep]/<camera>_<card>_<clip>`, the same layout as the bins. Clips are APFS clones of `RAW_COPY` (instant, no extra space; a hard link or copy on other drives). Cull rejects are left out, flagged clips sit in `00 Flagged/<reason>`, and each shaky Creative clip with a best cut is trimmed to it without re-encoding (`…_best_<in>-<out>`; the full clip stays in `RAW_COPY`). The originals and `RAW_COPY` are only read. `BY_CATEGORY/` is protected by the source-protection hook like `RAW_COPY/`. The map is in `edit/assemble/bycategory.json`, the log in `logs/bycategory.csv`.
- **Premiere relink** (bridge `relink`, in batches): project items point at their `BY_CATEGORY` clone. Same bytes, so subclips and sequence clips are unaffected. Trimmed clips stay on `RAW_COPY` so their ranges hold. Every bridge step still finds a clip by its `RAW_COPY` path after the relink.
- **Exposure before the look** (`assemble.js exposure`): every camera clip is measured in 8-bit on keyframes. A clip outside the look's average-brightness band from `kit/grade/targets.json` (Warm 55–150) gets a gentle Lumetri Basic Correction > Exposure fix, nudging it just inside the band, at most ±1.5 EV, and never clipping highlights. Low-key shots (dark with nothing bright, e.g. rings on black) are left alone and listed. Report: `edit/exposure_report.md`. The bridge `grade` step sets Exposure per clip before the look.
- **Premiere 26.x and LUTs:** a script can't load a LUT into Lumetri (a file path is refused with "Illegal Parameter type", and a list position doesn't load a user LUT; verified by exported frames). The grade step therefore sets Lumetri and the per-clip Exposure (tested: 479 clips, 0 failures). The editor picks Creative → Look → Buwan_* once on one clip, and `grade {look_from:"<sequence>/<index>"}` copies it to the rest (copy being verified). `/buwan:setup-kit` now installs the Buwan LUTs into Premiere's Creative look list (macOS `~/Library/Application Support/Adobe/Common/LUTs/Creative`, Windows `%APPDATA%\Adobe\Common\LUTs\Creative`); restart Premiere once. Grade and relink run in batches (`{start, limit}`), and relink also moves subclips (speech ranges) to `BY_CATEGORY`.
- **The basics now grade what they make:** bridge `basics` = import → organize → deadair → aroll → steady → relink → grade (exposure + look) on A-Roll clean and STABILIZE – Warp. The editor's own sequences are graded only when asked; Premiere can't script effects on bin clips. Hand-off note, house rules, basics, wedding-day, premiere-apply, README, MAINTAINING, session card and the pipeline pages were updated. Bridge `targetSequence` opens the sequence so QE effects land.

## buwan 1.6.0 · buwan-premiere 1.3.0 — 2026-11-08  (/plugin marketplace update buwan-films, then update both plugins and restart)
A new rule, so a minor version (it first went out as 1.5.2 / 1.2.2 in bf9be28; same content). The Premiere, Final Cut and Edit Pipeline artifacts now show it, and their pages are saved in `plugins/buwan/reference/`.
- **Steady — best cut first, stabilize gimbal shots only.** Every shaky Creative/Glider clip gets its best cut chosen first: a steady stretch (≥2.5 s) is a Steady pick and is not stabilized. Only **gimbal** shots with no steady stretch are stabilized, and only on their **best cut** (the least-shaky ~5 s, `BEST_CUT_S`), never the whole clip — Premiere places that range in `STABILIZE – Warp` with Warp Stabilizer; Final Cut gets keyword `stabilize` + a Favorite `stabilize` on the range. Handheld shaky shots are **not stabilized** (new decision `handheld`) and stay in `00 Flagged/Heavy motion` with their best cut in the reason.
- Gimbal shots: camera/folder name (`GIMBAL_CAMERAS`: Glider, Gimbal, Ronin, Steadi, Crane, Weebill, Zhiyun, RS 2/3/4), or listed per job in `edit/assemble/gimbal.txt` / `assemble.js steady --gimbal "1-Creative/5/" C0022 …` (`*` = the whole Creative camera). A "Creative" folder alone no longer counts as a gimbal. `steady_report.md` gains a Gimbal column. Skills, house rules, session card, README, MAINTAINING and the pipeline pages updated.

## buwan 1.5.1 · buwan-premiere 1.2.1 — 2026-11-08  (/plugin marketplace update buwan-films, then update both plugins and restart)
Fixes found on a real wedding SDE (Marynhor & Jomar, Premiere 26.5):
- **Clean speech WAVs were −12 LUFS / 0 dBTP, not −17 / −6:** ffmpeg `alimiter` auto-levels its output to 0 dBFS by default; now `level=disabled`. Clean WAVs are named by the clip's relative path (cameras and cards reuse `C0001…`, so bare names overwrote each other). Re-render existing clean WAVs; in Premiere, `changeMediaPath(samePath, true)` after re-rendering (refreshMedia leaves stale or silent conformed audio).
- **Premiere volume levels:** `Volume > Level` is 1.0 = +15 dB, 0.1778 = 0 dB; `dB()` now accounts for the offset (music "−20 dB" was landing at ≈−5 dB, intro "−3 dB" at ≈+12 dB, camera gains clamped at +15).
- **Premiere build:** b-roll audio goes on its own muted "B-roll nat sound" track (it was overwriting the A1 speech); added audio tracks are stereo (`addTracks` type 1; type 2 is 5.1 and pushed music onto other tracks in pieces).
- **Build / slow-mo:** speech selects and `realtime_clips` (e.g. multicam angles) are never slowed — Premiere interprets slow-mo project-wide, so slowing a clip broke its speech and SYNC uses. New build options: `cut_targets`, `bursts` (editor-set burst windows), `part_broll`, `peak_broll`, `final_hold_clip`.
- **Cull:** frames forced to 8-bit (`format=yuv420p`) so thresholds hold on 10-bit Sony footage; blur falls back to the ffmpeg ≥ 9 `blur mean:` summary.
- **Sync:** silent clips (< −55 dBFS) aren't matched; every candidate is verified by envelope correlation (r ≥ 0.4); a per-camera clock error is learned from verified matches and used to place the rest — no fallback to untrusted camera clocks.

## buwan 1.5.0 · buwan-premiere 1.2.0 — 2026-11-08  (/plugin marketplace update buwan-films; Premiere editors reinstall buwan-premiere)
- **The basics are the only automatic goal for a new wedding project** (`/buwan:basics`, Premiere `/buwan-premiere:wedding-day`, bridge step `basics`): dedupe (working copy) · cull · categorize · dead air + A-Roll clean · label colors · shaky Creative/Glider footage. The editor makes the project and places the music by hand. No multicam sync, no story, no cut, no sequence to cut in unless asked. Two pipeline pages, one per app: Premiere Pro and Final Cut Pro (links in the README).
- **Working copy on the drive** (`assemble.js workcopy`, `/buwan:intake --work-copy`): the raw clips mirrored into `<raw>_WORK/RAW_COPY/` next to the raw folder on the same SSD (`PROJECT/`, `AUDIO_CLEAN/`, `EXPORTS/`, `logs/` beside it), free space checked first, every file verified by SHA-256 (`logs/copy_manifest.csv`), **byte-identical duplicates skipped** and logged (`logs/dedupe_report.md`) so a card copied twice or a `z/` backup never imports twice. `edit/SOURCES.md` points at the copy; originals and copy go on `~/.buwan/protected-paths.txt`, enforced by the source-protection hook alongside `sources/` and any `RAW_COPY/`. `scan --dedupe` / `assemble.js dedupe` for reference-in-place jobs.
- **Steady** (`assemble.js steady`, bridge `steady`): shaky clips on the Creative/Glider camera only — the longest steady stretch (≥2.5 s) becomes a **Steady pick** (Premiere subclip in `<category>/Steady picks`; Final Cut Favorite `steady` + keyword `steady pick`); no steady stretch → **Warp Stabilizer** (Premiere: placed in the `STABILIZE – Warp` sequence with the effect applied; Final Cut: keyword `stabilize`, tick Stabilization); too shaky → `00 Flagged/Heavy motion`. Shaky clips on other cameras are left alone.
- **Flagged, not rejected:** soft-focus, dark, blown and frozen b-roll (and heavy motion the steady step couldn't save) is parked in `00 Flagged/<reason>` in Premiere, category label kept, reason in Description. Speech is never flagged. `--no-flags` / `{flag_warnings:false}` turns it off.
- **`/buwan:multicam`** — sync by audio is now a skill the editor asks for (vows, interviews, ceremony); removed from the automatic run.
- `assemble.js handoff` writes `EDITOR_START_HERE.md` in the WORK folder. Cull reads ffmpeg metadata from stdout as well as stderr (motion/blur metrics were empty on some ffmpeg builds). FCPXML favorites can carry a name. House rules, `/buwan:sde`, premiere-apply, session card, README, MAINTAINING, Edit Pipeline pages updated.
