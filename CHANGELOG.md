# Changelog

## buwan 1.5.2 · buwan-premiere 1.2.2 — 2026-11-08  (/plugin marketplace update buwan-films, then update both plugins and restart)
- **Steady — best cut first, stabilize gimbal shots only.** Every shaky Creative/Glider clip gets its best cut chosen first: a steady stretch (≥2.5 s) is a Steady pick and is not stabilized. Only **gimbal** shots with no steady stretch are stabilized, and only on their **best cut** (the least-shaky ~5 s, `BEST_CUT_S`), never the whole clip — Premiere places that range in `STABILIZE – Warp` with Warp Stabilizer; Final Cut gets keyword `stabilize` + a Favorite `stabilize` on the range. Handheld shaky shots are **not stabilized** (new decision `handheld`) and stay in `00 Flagged/Heavy motion` with their best cut in the reason.
- Gimbal shots: camera/folder name (`GIMBAL_CAMERAS`: Glider, Gimbal, Ronin, Steadi, Crane, Weebill, Zhiyun, RS 2/3/4), or listed per job in `edit/assemble/gimbal.txt` / `assemble.js steady --gimbal "1-Creative/5/" C0022 …` (`*` = the whole Creative camera). A "Creative" folder alone no longer counts as a gimbal. `steady_report.md` gains a Gimbal column. Skills, house rules, session card, README, MAINTAINING and the pipeline pages updated.

## buwan 1.5.1 · buwan-premiere 1.2.1 — 2026-11-08  (/plugin marketplace update buwan-films, then update both plugins and restart)
Fixes found on a real wedding SDE (Marynhor & Jomar, Premiere 26.5):
- **Clean speech WAVs were −12 LUFS / 0 dBTP, not −17 / −6:** ffmpeg `alimiter` auto-levels its output to 0 dBFS by default; now `level=disabled`. Clean WAVs are named by the clip's relative path (cameras and cards reuse `C0001…`, so bare names overwrote each other). Re-render existing clean WAVs; in Premiere, `changeMediaPath(samePath, true)` after re-rendering (refreshMedia leaves stale or silent conformed audio).
- **Premiere volume levels:** `Volume > Level` is 1.0 = +15 dB, 0.1778 = 0 dB; `dB()` now accounts for the offset (music "−20 dB" was landing at ≈−5 dB, intro "−3 dB" at ≈+12 dB, camera gains clamped at +15).
- **Premiere build:** b-roll audio goes on its own muted "B-roll nat sound" track (it was overwriting the A1 speech); added audio tracks are stereo (`addTracks` type 1; type 2 is 5.1 and pushed music onto other tracks in pieces).
- **Build / slow-mo:** speech selects and `realtime_clips` (e.g. multicam angles) are never slowed — Premiere interprets slow-mo project-wide, so slowing a clip broke its speech and SYNC uses. New build options: `cut_targets`, `bursts` (editor-set burst windows), `part_broll`, `peak_broll`, `final_hold_clip`.
- **Cull:** frames forced to 8-bit (`format=yuv420p`) so thresholds hold on 10-bit Sony footage; blur falls back to the ffmpeg ≥ 9 `blur mean:` summary.
- **Sync:** silent clips (< −55 dBFS) aren't matched; every candidate is verified by envelope correlation (r ≥ 0.4); a per-camera clock error is learned from verified matches and used to place the rest — no fallback to untrusted camera clocks.

## buwan 1.5.0 · buwan-premiere 1.2.0 — 2026-11-08  (/plugin marketplace update buwan-films; Premiere editors reinstall buwan-premiere)
- **The basics are the only automatic goal for a new wedding project** (`/buwan:basics`, Premiere `/buwan-premiere:wedding-day`, bridge step `basics`): dedupe (working copy) · cull · categorize · dead air + A-Roll clean · label colors · shaky Creative/Glider footage. The editor makes the project and places the music by hand. No multicam sync, no story, no cut, no sequence to cut in unless asked. Two pipeline pages, one per app: Premiere Pro and Final Cut Pro (links in the README).
- **Working copy on the drive** (`assemble.js workcopy`, `/buwan:intake --work-copy`): the raw clips mirrored into `<raw>_WORK/RAW_COPY/` next to the raw folder on the same SSD (`PROJECT/`, `AUDIO_CLEAN/`, `EXPORTS/`, `logs/` beside it), free space checked first, every file verified by SHA-256 (`logs/copy_manifest.csv`), **byte-identical duplicates skipped** and logged (`logs/dedupe_report.md`) so a card copied twice or a `z/` backup never imports twice. `edit/SOURCES.md` points at the copy; originals and copy go on `~/.buwan/protected-paths.txt`, enforced by the source-protection hook alongside `sources/` and any `RAW_COPY/`. `scan --dedupe` / `assemble.js dedupe` for reference-in-place jobs.
- **Steady** (`assemble.js steady`, bridge `steady`): shaky clips on the Creative/Glider camera only — the longest steady stretch (≥2.5 s) becomes a **Steady pick** (Premiere subclip in `<category>/Steady picks`; Final Cut Favorite `steady` + keyword `steady pick`); no steady stretch → **Warp Stabilizer** (Premiere: placed in the `STABILIZE – Warp` sequence with the effect applied; Final Cut: keyword `stabilize`, tick Stabilization); too shaky → `00 Flagged/Heavy motion`. Shaky clips on other cameras are left alone.
- **Flagged, not rejected:** soft-focus, dark, blown and frozen b-roll (and heavy motion the steady step couldn't save) is parked in `00 Flagged/<reason>` in Premiere, category label kept, reason in Description. Speech is never flagged. `--no-flags` / `{flag_warnings:false}` turns it off.
- **`/buwan:multicam`** — sync by audio is now a skill the editor asks for (vows, interviews, ceremony); removed from the automatic run.
- `assemble.js handoff` writes `EDITOR_START_HERE.md` in the WORK folder. Cull reads ffmpeg metadata from stdout as well as stderr (motion/blur metrics were empty on some ffmpeg builds). FCPXML favorites can carry a name. House rules, `/buwan:sde`, premiere-apply, session card, README, MAINTAINING, Edit Pipeline pages updated.
