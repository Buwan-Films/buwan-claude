# Buwan studio pages

The claude.ai pages the team works from. The rules live in this repository; these pages are the visual versions for people. A copy of each page is kept in `plugins/buwan/reference/` (see [MAINTAINING.md](../MAINTAINING.md) for how to update them). Pages open only for people the owner has shared them with.

## Start here
| Page | What it is for | Who uses it | Copy in the repo |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Buwan Films Workflow](https://claude.ai/artifact/XspJWQPLCpuhofjkyzse7d) | The whole studio flow: plan → shoot → edit → deliver, for wedding, prenup, corporate and commercial / reels, with each role's steps (owner, lead shooter, shooters, BTS, data wrangler, editor) | Everyone | `reference/studio-flow.html` |
| [Buwan Storyboard](https://claude.ai/artifact/LJpY3x7t19hgkKZuE1Ed9D) | Plan a film before the shoot: brief, music, beats, cards, the shot list per camera (ticked on the day), the social plan for BTS. Exports the bundle that goes into `edit/storyboard/` | Owner, editor, lead shooter, shooters, BTS | `reference/storyboard.html` |
