Buwan Video Kit

Video Component Kit

Every element a Buwan Films wedding film needs — titles, lower thirds, transitions, social frames — built to the brand. Each preview is a real 1920×1080 frame scaled to fit, so spec sizes are the pixel values you set in your editor at 1080p. Click any frame to replay its motion. Toggle Editing in the bar to restyle any element — click it, then recolor, resize, nudge, rewrite or hide it — and Download clip saves the frame (with your edits) as a self-contained animated HTML file you can open full-screen and screen-record into your edit.

Fonts. Names and headings are Cormorant Garamond (the website face), letterspaced labels are Cinzel, and the accent script is Italiana — all free on Google Fonts, loaded live here. Motion is slow and quiet: long crossfades, rising gold hairlines, letter-tracking reveals. Nothing pops; everything breathes. Grade footage in your Kodak/Dehancer look first, then lay these on top.
