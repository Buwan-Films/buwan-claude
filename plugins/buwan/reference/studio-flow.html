Plan, shoot, edit, deliver

Every Buwan film, from the client brief to the reels. The storyboard is the plan, and its shot list goes to the shooters. What happens on the day decides the story, and the gates still apply. Pick a shoot type, then a role to see only that person’s steps.

Standard step On request Reads the storyboard (proposed) Gate

The shot list

Built from the storyboard, so nobody types it twice. One list per camera, must-haves first, opened on the shooters' phones. Each tick records the clip name, so the rough cut knows where every planned shot lives. Example below: a wedding.

Faster edit, same quality

Most edit time goes to waiting and to work that could happen earlier. Move everything that does not depend on the footage into planning, and keep every gate.
