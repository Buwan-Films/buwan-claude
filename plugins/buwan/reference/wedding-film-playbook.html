Storyboard

A wedding storyboard is a story plan, a list of must-get shots and a map for the edit. You can't redo the vows, so storyboard the SDE or highlights film; the full ceremony is multicam coverage. Build it on the Buwan Storyboard page and start from its wedding shot flow.

Shot cards

One card per key shot. On the Storyboard page, add a phone photo from your ocular visit at the real angle to the beat.

How to build it

  1. Find the storyInterview the couple before the wedding. Look for the hook: how they met, an inside joke, the quiet dad who will cry. "From jeepney to altar" can open on a jeepney shot.
  2. Choose the two tracksTrack 1 carries the intro, track 2 the story to the end. Mark the drop for peak 1 and the energy peak for peak 2. The voice comes from the letters first, then vows and speeches. Never write lines ahead: the real words are picked at Story-Lock.
  3. Lay out the beatsStart from the wedding shot flow on the Storyboard page: the SDE shape above, each beat marked speaker, b-roll, card or music only, and hold or burst.
  4. Give every b-roll beat its shotWhat we see, shot type, camera (Creative / Glider, Main, 3rd Cam, Drone, Audio, BTS) and must-have. That becomes the shot list.
  5. Add visualsStick figures and arrows are enough. Pinterest or ShotDeck references help. Photos from the actual church are best.
  6. Merge with the run of showPut every shot on the day's timeline and make sure each camera can get its must-haves; agree lanes with the photographer.
  7. Tag prioritiesMUST, SHOULD, BONUS. When the day runs late, chase only MUST.
  8. Share the Shot listThe Storyboard page's Shot list view, grouped by camera, on every shooter's phone. They tick shots and note clip names on the day. Brief the crew the day before, at the venue if you can.
  9. Edit from the storyboardExport the bundle into the job's edit/storyboard/. /buwan:plot puts the real transcript into the planned beats, /buwan:rough-cut pulls b-roll by the ticked shots, and the proof gate measures the cut.

Tools

  • Buwan Storyboard: beats, music, cards, the shot list per camera and the BTS social plan, saved for the whole team
  • Board view on that page: the visual preview to show the couple
  • Paper + 16:9 template: fastest for brainstorming before you type it in

Habits that pay off

  • Keep a master template. About 70% of every Catholic wedding follows the same flow.
  • Leave free slots for the unplanned: Lolo crying, the ring bearer tripping.
  • Show the couple a mood board, not the full storyboard.
  • After each wedding, note what you got, what you missed and what to add.

Before the day

  • Ocular visit; ask the coordinator or sacristan about parish rules on altar access, flash and fixed spots.
  • Get the vows script and the program or missalette.
  • Client sign-off on the storyboard, the music, the names and the social plan.
  • Brief the crew the day before with the Shot list. Every camera: S-Cinetone 10-bit (8-bit bodies: Cine4 + Pro color), white balance in Kelvin, never auto.
  • Shoot photos of the real angles for the storyboard frames.

On the day

  • Sync timecode or clap on every camera before the ceremony.
  • Check the groom's lav before the entrance and again before the vows.
  • Program running late? Switch to MUST shots only.
  • Tick shots on the Shot list and note the clip names.
  • Every card: two copies on two drives before anything else. Never format a card until the job is closed.
  • The data wrangler runs /buwan:basics on the first cards and /buwan:sync on each new one, and tells you which must-haves are still missing.

The SDE shape

OPEN COLD

Four shots with no faces, then the letter as the first voice. No title at the start.

TWO PEAKS

First look and entourage at ~1:00; entrance, reception and the kiss at ~75 %. Quiet valleys between them, with the vows held.

END ON A HOLD

25–30 s on one shot with the last vow lines, then the wedding title and the logo end card.

Edit tricks

  • Use the real vows audio as part of the soundtrack.
  • Match cut: the bride's hand at prep to her hand at the altar.
  • Sound design: church bells, coins, applause, laughter.
  • Mix real time with slow motion so it feels alive.
  • Keep the SDE to 4–7 minutes.

In the Buwan flow

  • /buwan:basics: the enhanced clips (exposure and white balance fixed, shaky glider shots smoothed, no grade), then /buwan:sync for each new card, with transcripts.
  • /buwan:multicam for the vows and speeches, on request.
  • /buwan:plot → /buwan:rough-cut → proof gate, from the storyboard bundle. Or /buwan:sde for the whole thing compressed.
  • Film the couple watching the SDE: it's the ending of the full film.
