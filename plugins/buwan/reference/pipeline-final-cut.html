Before · you, by hand

Hand over the footage

  1. The raw clips on the SSD, one subfolder per shooter: 1-Creative (or Glider), 2-Main, 3-3rd Cam, 4-Drone, Audio. Claude only ever reads them.
  2. Shot in S-Cinetone (10-bit bodies) or Cine4 + Pro color (8-bit), white balance in Kelvin.
  3. Room on the SSD: about 0.6 GB per minute of footage.
  4. Start Claude Code in your Buwan workspace.
The basics · Claude Code · one run, unattended

Enhanced clips, then stop

  1. Exposure on every clip — measured and gently fixed toward the studio brightness band (±1.5 EV at most, highlights protected; low-key shots like rings on black left alone).
  2. White balance on every clip — the colour cast read from neutral surfaces (gowns, barong, walls) and gently corrected: natural warmth kept, blue casts and green/magenta tint pulled back (½ stop at most). Drone shots and mixed light left alone. The camera's highlights above white are kept, so the grade can still pull them back.
  3. Shaky — Creative/Glider only — the best part of each shaky shot, with a smooth glide (vid.stab). Other cameras are left alone.
The raw folder is never touched · a new folder every run · no grade · no copy · no dedupe · no sorting
Only when you ask

Separate skills

  • Interviews · vows · speeches — /buwan:multicam: you give the clip names of one moment (C0412 C0201 ZOOM0007); the best audio is picked and cleaned (−17 LUFS), the angles synced to it, the dead air cut on every angle → <raw>_SPEECH_…/INTERVIEWS · VOWS · SPEECHES.
  • Grade — /buwan:grade: the studio look, built from the studio's own film, applied in Final Cut.
  • Story — /buwan:plot. Cut — /buwan:sde.
  • Couple's names — /buwan:title "Name and Name": “The Wedding of”, the names wide-tracked in Cinzel, Buwan Films, over the last wide shot (kit 07.09).
  • Logo end card — /buwan:end-card: the last shot of every film, after the wedding title: two blades become one ring, the Buwan emblem lands in a burst of crescent moons (kit 06.08, 7.7 s).
  • Finish — /buwan:graphics · /buwan:sfx · /buwan:export.

Can I run the skills for Final Cut? Yes, every skill. You type the prompts in Claude Code; the skills make the clips, the FCPXML timelines and the graphics. Claude can't work inside the open Final Cut library (there is no bridge like Premiere's), so the last step is always yours: an import, a Connect or an Append, one or two clicks. A timeline from Claude always comes in as a new project; your edits are never touched. Final Cut questions: /buwan:final-cut.

The film ends: last wide shot with The Wedding of · the names · Buwan Films (07.09) → dip to black → the logo end card (06.08). Same day with no time: /buwan:sde 2026-11-15_Santos-Reyes runs steps 4–10 compressed.

The prompt /buwan:basics 2026-11-15_Santos-Reyes --footage /Volumes/T7/Santos-Reyes

New cards from the shooters during the day: /buwan:sync 2026-11-15_Santos-Reyes — only the new clips, into the same enhanced folder, then every clip with speech transcribed locally (about 35× real time) into edit/transcripts/. The raw is still untouched.

Shared by every editor through the buwan Claude Code plugin (github.com/Buwan-Films/buwan-claude). The raw footage is read-only to Claude: every run writes new clips next to it. The story and the cut are yours.
