Before · you, by hand

Make the project

  1. Hand the raw clips over on the SSD, one subfolder per shooter: 1-Main, 2-Creative (or Glider), 3-Reactions, 4-Drone, Audio.
  2. New library on the SSD next to <raw>_WORK/, media left in place (Leave files in place), cache on the SSD.
  3. Drop your music in where you want it. Claude Code doesn't touch it.
  4. Start Claude Code in your Buwan workspace.
The basics · Claude Code · one run, unattended

Six things, then stop

  1. Dedupe + working copy — the raw clips mirrored into <raw>_WORK/RAW_COPY/ on the same SSD, every file verified by SHA-256, byte-identical duplicates skipped and logged (logs/dedupe_report.md). Only the copy is read from here on.
  2. Cull — false starts, black, blown and dead clips rejected. Flags, never deletions.
  3. Categorize — every clip into one of the 12 studio categories (Speech, Prep & Details, Portraits, Ceremony, Reactions & BTS, Establishing, Reception & Party, Drone, Music, SFX, Graphics, Grade & Adjust). Prep also gets a Bride prep or Groom prep keyword from one frame per clip; objects on their own keep only Prep & Details.
  4. Dead air — the speech inside every speech clip found, silences cut; cleaned −17 LUFS WAVs in AUDIO_CLEAN/; the A-Roll clean is the whole day's speech back to back.
  5. Label colors — a Role per category (Dialogue, Details, Portraits, Ceremony, Reactions, Establishing, Party, Drone, Music) and a Keyword Collection per bin, delivered as one validated FCPXML. Set the role colors once: Modify → Edit Roles. Soft-focus, dark, blown and frozen b-roll parked in the warn keyword with the reason in the clip note (rejects carry the Rejected rating), label kept.
  6. Shaky — Creative/Glider only — the longest steady stretch (≥2.5 s) becomes a Steady pick (the best cut is always chosen first); no steady stretch on a gimbal shot → keyword stabilize + a Favorite on its best cut; you tick Stabilization in the Inspector; handheld or nothing to save → Flagged, not stabilized. Shaky clips on other cameras are left alone.
Stops here · no sync · no story · no cut · no music
Only when you ask

Separate skills

  • Multicam — /buwan:multicam: vows, interviews, ceremony synced by audio; wrong camera clocks don't matter.
  • Story — /buwan:plot: transcribe the A-Roll clean (Taglish verbatim), tag beats, pick lines.
  • Cut — the music-driven timeline, proof gate, grade, levels: /buwan:sde.
  • Finish — /buwan:graphics · /buwan:sfx · /buwan:export.
