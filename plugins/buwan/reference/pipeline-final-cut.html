Before · you, by hand

Hand over the footage

  1. The raw clips on the SSD, one subfolder per shooter: 1-Main, 2-Creative (or Glider), 3-Reactions, 4-Drone, Audio. Claude only ever reads them.
  2. Name the studio film the look should match, the first time (e.g. your last SDE).
  3. Room on the SSD: about 0.6 GB per minute of footage.
  4. Start Claude Code in your Buwan workspace.
The basics · Claude Code · one run, unattended

Enhanced clips, then stop

  1. Dead air + speech audio — interviews, letters, speeches and vows cut down to their speech (silences out, soft joins) on cleaned audio levelled to −17 LUFS, peaks −6 dBFS. A speech clip with silent camera audio comes through whole and is flagged.
  2. Exposure on every clip — measured and gently fixed toward the look's brightness band (±1.5 EV at most, highlights protected; low-key shots like rings on black left alone).
  3. The studio look — a LUT built to match this wedding to the studio's own film: soft film contrast, whites that stay white, color by shadows, mids and highlights. You see a before / after sheet first.
  4. Shaky — Creative/Glider only — the best part of each shaky shot, with a smooth glide (vid.stab). Other cameras are left alone.
The raw folder is never touched · a new folder every run · no copy · no dedupe · no sorting
Only when you ask

Separate skills

  • Multicam — /buwan:multicam: vows, interviews, ceremony synced by audio.
  • Story — /buwan:plot: transcribe the speech (Taglish verbatim), tag beats, pick lines.
  • Cut — the music-driven timeline and the proof gate: /buwan:sde.
  • Finish — /buwan:graphics · /buwan:sfx · /buwan:export.
