01 · Shape

Two peaks, two valleys, one long exhale

The film doesn't build once and release. It bursts early on the entourage, drops into dialogue, bursts again on the reception and the kiss, then holds a silhouette for half a minute while the groom finishes his vow. The cut rate follows the music exactly — read the chart as the shape of the song.

02 · Beat by beat

What happens, when, and why it works

0:00–0:13 · Cold open · no title, no speech

Arrival before anything

Car wheel, empty church pews, groom alone at the altar, bouquet in hand. Four 3-second shots, music barely there. The film starts on "before", not on the couple.

0:13–0:26 · Bride's letter VO opens

The letter is the spine

First voice heard is the bride reading her letter — not the priest, not the MC. It plays over a B&W close-up of her smiling and the groom in a doorway.

"Adrian, it's funny to think that way back in 2021, when we first met in Canada, I had no idea that the person standing in front of me today would become the love of my life."
0:26–1:10 · Peak 1 · 2.2s avg · music +12 dB

The hook burst

First look (0:36), top-down twirl of the gown (0:42), groomsmen, the whole entourage cheering, rings and invitation insert right on the drop (1:06). Peak lands at 1:00. This is the "would you keep watching" test, passed by 1:10.

1:10–1:20 · Drop-out · music −17 dB

Silence after the burst

Veil portrait, church wide from the back, the ring bearer. Three slow shots. The film earns the next section by going quiet.

1:16–2:22 · "I do" intercut with the letter

Ceremony and letter braided, not sequenced

The priest's question and the "Yes, I do" are cut in between lines of the bride's letter — the ceremony audio is a punctuation mark inside her story, not a scene of its own. Visuals: groom reading his letter in the suite (1:48), hugs (2:06), the altar.

"Who would have thought that the guy I barely noticed would eventually become the man I would choose to spend the rest of my life with?"
2:22–3:15 · Build 2 · priest's blessing · music −7 dB

Celebration montage under the homily

The priest's words ("make your home your happy place… God will bless you more") carry a celebration run: entourage cheering outside the church, couple portrait, group photos on the steps, the first kiss at 3:00. Energy is up but never at burst speed.

3:15–3:38 · Valley · −15 dB · no speech

Reactions, then tears

Bridesmaids watching, mother and daughter in B&W (3:18), bride wiping a tear (3:48). Breathing room before the heaviest beat.

3:38–4:46 · Groom's vow · 3.4–4s shots · music −15 dB

The centrepiece is held, not cut

Two-shot of the couple reading vows in the garden, held for 3–4 seconds at a time, cutaways only to her in blue (4:18) and a silhouette (4:30). Longest holds in the film outside the ending: 7.5s at 3:18, 8.3s at 3:31, 8.4s at 4:49. No b-roll churn under the vow.

"Five years ago, in October, I stood in this very church. I was 25 and I was scared. I prayed for an angel… Today, October 8, 2026, exactly five years after — I'm standing in the same church again, but this time I'm not alone."
4:46–5:50 · Peak 2 · the burst · 1.5s avg · 20 cuts in 30s

Everything lands on the second peak

The music climbs from 4:40 and tops out 5:10–5:40. Bride's entrance (4:54–5:06), reception venue reveal (5:12), first dance (5:24), parents (5:36), and the altar kiss at 5:54 — placed here, out of order, so the kiss sits on the music's peak. The groom's "I choose you always" spans the drop.

5:50–6:52 · Resolve · 28s final hold

Silhouette, final vow lines, then the title

B&W tears (6:06), church doors, the walk out (6:18), then a single night silhouette held 27.8 seconds while the groom finishes: "I promise to spend the rest of my life walking beside you. My wife, my angel, I love you." Title card — The Wedding of Adrian and Lara — at 6:42, studio logo at 6:48. The title closes the film; it doesn't open it.

04 · Rules for /buwan:sde

What to take from it

  • Open cold, on "before." Four 3-second shots with no faces of the couple: a vehicle, the empty venue, a detail, one person alone. No title, no speech, music under.Reference: 0:00–0:13.
  • The first voice is a letter, not a priest. Bride's or groom's letter opens the speech spine at ~0:13 and returns throughout. Ceremony audio ("do you take… I do") is cut in between letter lines as punctuation, never played as its own block.Reference: 0:13 and 1:16–2:22.
  • Build to two peaks, not one. Peak 1 at ~1:00 on the entourage and first look (2.2s cuts, rings/invite insert on the drop). Peak 2 at ~75% of runtime on entrance → reception → kiss (1.5s cuts, 20 in 30s). Between them, two dialogue valleys at −15 dB where the cut rate halves.Reference: 0:26–1:10 and 4:46–5:50. Adjust /buwan:sde's structure from one climb to two.
  • Go silent right after the first burst. Three slow shots, music dropped ~17 dB, before the ceremony starts. The valley makes the next section land.Reference: 1:10–1:20.
  • Hold the vow. Two-shot of the couple, 3–4s per shot, at most two cutaways (her reaction, a silhouette). No b-roll churn under the vow lines. This is the emotional centre and it sits in the quietest part of the film.Reference: 3:38–4:46.
  • Move the kiss to peak 2. Chronology bends for the music: the altar kiss goes after the reception footage so it lands on the loudest bar. The pipeline already says this — the reference proves it reads naturally.Reference: kiss at 5:54.
  • Three B&W shots, intimate only. A smile in prep, a parent's embrace, a tear. Never a wide, never the entourage.Reference: 0:24, 3:18, 6:06.
  • End on one long hold with the last vow line. Night silhouette or equivalent, 25–30 seconds, no cuts, final 2–3 lines of the vow over it. Then the title card and the logo. The title closes the film.Reference: 6:24–6:52. This moves the title plate from "first hit" (pipeline) to the end for SDEs — keep "first hit" for highlight films if you prefer, but the reference's way makes the SDE feel like a film, not a slideshow.
  • Numbers for the proof gate. Avg shot 2.5–3s · burst ≥15 cuts/30s at ~1.5s · vow shots 3–4s · final hold 25–30s · two music peaks · title in the last 10s.All measured from the reference; swap into /buwan:proof-gate for SDE mode.
