00 · Shoot day · field

Coverage — what the edit can use

Prep → ceremony → post-nuptial → reception. Vows, letters, speeches and reception messages are the speech spine; reactions and BTS are the cutaway bank for fast-cut bursts; façades and empty venues are the breathing room. Photo and video teams move together; equipment check after every phase.

Source

Raw footage & audio

Camera files, lav/recorder WAVs of vows, letters, and speeches. Originals never touched.

01 · Automatic

Intake

Copy, verify, organize by ceremony segment. Sources stay read-only.

02 · AI cut

Rough cut — Story-Lock

Lock the story before the edit, never find it in the edit. Transcribe speech once, tag every line into the audio layers below, pick the strongest lines, order for emotional build. Music is chosen after the audio — the audio leads, the music follows, tuned to the day's atmosphere. Rebuilt as real, trimmable clips — never a flat render.

2a · Tag the 8 audio layers→ 2b · Music after audio→ 2c · Build the 4-part structure→ 2d · B-roll last, serving the story

03 · Measured

Audio polish

Dialogue to −17 LUFS, peaks −6 dBFS. Clean speech is the spine of the film.

04 · Camera only

Grade

Wedding: muted filmic look — warm/naturalistic or cool/desaturated per the palette, B&W for the most intimate beats. Corporate: clean, brand-true grade; crimson reserved for award beats. Reels/raw screen-rec skip it.

07 · You

Review — taste only

Everything measurable already passed its gate. This pass is pacing feel and emotional build — wedding holds go to vows and first looks; corporate holds go to speakers and the keynote's key line.
