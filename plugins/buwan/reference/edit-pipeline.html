The Edit Pipeline

End-to-end post-production flow for wedding SDEs, highlight films, and corporate event videos. The footage is prepared by machine, the story is locked by hand, the cut is timed to the music you choose — and every cut is measured before it moves on. Runs on the editor's own Mac or PC; nothing is uploaded.

00 · Shoot day · field

Coverage — what the edit can use

Prep → ceremony → post-nuptial → reception. Vows, letters, speeches and reception messages are the speech spine; reactions and BTS are the cutaway bank for fast-cut bursts; façades and empty venues are the breathing room. Photo and video teams move together; equipment check after every phase.

Source

Raw footage, audio & your two tracks

Camera files (any mix of cameras and frame rates), lav/recorder WAVs of vows, letters and speeches, and a music/ folder with 01 · intro and 02 · story — the editor chooses the songs. Originals are locked read-only and never touched.

01 · Automatic

Intake — the raw folder, read only

Same-day: the raw clips stay where the shooters put them, one subfolder per shooter, and Claude only reads them. Enhanced clips go into a new folder next to the raw on every run (<raw>_ENHANCED_<date-time>/); nothing is copied, deduplicated or moved. Highlights and features: /buwan:intake sets up the project folder.

01b · Automatic · the assemble engine

Assemble — the footage prepares itself

One run, on the editor's machine, deterministic: same footage in, same result out. Rejects are flags, never deletions; the editor can un-reject anything by name. /buwan:assemble

Scan · probe every file→ Cull · rejects the unusable→ Steady · shaky Creative/Glider shots: best cut, smooth glide→ Exposure · fixed on every clip→ Render · <raw>_ENHANCED_…, no grade→ On request · multicam: interviews, vows, speeches→ On request · grade: the studio look in the app
Interviews, vows, speeches — /buwan:multicam, on request

The editor lists the clips of one moment — every angle plus the lav or recorder file. The best audio is picked (signal-to-noise, clipping, level) and the other angles are synced to it by sound. That audio is cleaned (noise reduction, voice EQ) to −17 LUFS, peaks −6, and the dead air between words is cut at the same points on every angle — words never cut. Each angle is rendered with its exposure fixed and starts at its timecode in the cut moment, into <raw>_SPEECH_<date-time>/INTERVIEWS · VOWS · SPEECHES/<moment>/: the multicam is built by timecode in any app.

A new wedding project: the basics, then stop

Claude Code makes enhanced clips and stops. It reads the raw folder and writes a new one next to it on every run, <raw>_ENHANCED_<date-time>/, same folders and names: exposure fixed on every clip; shaky Creative/Glider shots cut to their best part with a smooth glide; the original audio. No grade, no speech work, no copying, no dedupe, no sorting, no story, no cut. Per-app hand-off: Premiere Pro · Final Cut Pro. /buwan:basics

Sync by sound, not by clock

Cameras are matched on their audio, so a camera whose clock was never set still lands to the frame. In /buwan:multicam every angle of a moment is synced to its best audio. Bins and colors (the 12 studio categories, the same in Premiere, Final Cut and Resolve) are applied only when the editor asks.

Imports as

Folders of HEVC 10-bit clips that any app imports as they are: _ENHANCED_ (camera and card folders become bins) and _SPEECH_ (one folder per moment; angles → multicam by timecode). Bins, labels and keywords only when the editor asks (validated FCPXML for Final Cut and Resolve; the bridge for Premiere).

02 · AI cut

Rough cut — Story-Lock

Lock the story before the edit, never find it in the edit. Transcribe speech once (Taglish kept verbatim), tag every line into the audio layers below, pick the strongest lines, order for emotional build. The audio leads, the music follows — the editor's two tracks become the clock the film is cut to. Rebuilt as real, trimmable clips — never a flat render. /buwan:plot → /buwan:rough-cut

2a · Tag the 8 audio layers→ 2b · Music after audio→ 2c · Build the 4-part structure→ 2d · B-roll last, cut to the music
The music-driven cut · two tracks
Track 1 · Intro · b-roll on bars · title plate on the first hitTrack 2 · Story → Ceremony → Outro

Cuts land on beats, long shots on bars. The cut rate follows the music's energy: ~3 s shots when quiet, ~2 s as it builds, 1–2 s in the burst montage on the peak. Breathing room is added at part boundaries so the kiss lands on the music's peak. Vows and first looks are holds — no cutaways. The speaker stays on camera for the opening of each part. Slow motion is conformed. The outro resolves on the track's tail; the logo reveal lands before the final beat. Track 1 plays at −3 dB; track 2 sits under dialogue as a −20 dB bed.

The SDE shape · measured from the reference film
Cold open · 4 × 3 s · "before"Letter opens the spine · 0:13Peak 1 · ~1:00 · first look, entourageValley · music −17 dB"I do" as punctuationVow held · 3–4 s shotsPeak 2 · ~75 % · entrance → reception → kissFinal hold · 25–30 sTitle + logo close the film

Taken apart from the Adrian & Lara reference (6:52, 146 cuts, 2.8 s average, two music peaks). Two peaks, two dialogue valleys, one long exhale. The engine builds this with title_at: end and final_hold_s: 27; the gate measures it with --mode sde. Full breakdown: Buwan SDE Reference.

What comes out

The timeline as FCPXML (speech on the primary storyline, b-roll connected above, music on the lanes below, markers for parts, holds, the peak and the graphics to render) · a picture EDL for the proof gate · an edit map — one picture of the whole cut to review before importing.

Film structure · Wedding
Intro · 60–90s · hookStoryline · 2–2.5 min · contextCeremony · climaxOutro · 2–3 min · resolution

The intro passes the 5–10 second test — would you keep watching? No strong welcome remarks or power statements → let the music carry it. B-roll goes on only after the story is locked: every shot matches or elevates the audio's emotion, never decoration.

Proof gate — every cut measured, automatically, on every EDL: average shot 2.5–3 s, ≥15 cuts in the 30 s burst, holds of 15–20 s, no flash frames. Corporate: steady 1.5–2.5 s, holds on speakers. On-beat and on-word timing is confirmed by eye.

03 · Measured

Audio polish

Dialogue to −17 LUFS, peaks −6 dBFS, measured on the export. Clean speech is the spine of the film. /buwan:audio

04 · Camera only

Grade

Applied in the editing app when the editor asks — never baked in by the basics, which only fix the exposure. The studio look is built from the studio's own film (a .cube + before/after sheet), or one of Buwan_Warm · Buwan_Cool · Buwan_BW — the same file in every app. Shoot S-Cinetone; no Log on same-day jobs. B&W only for the most intimate beats. Every graded export is measured by the grade check against the studio targets. Corporate: clean, brand-true; crimson reserved for award beats. /buwan:grade

05 · Graphics + captions

Graphics & captions

5a · Plan — storyboard every beat→ 5b · Build — render & place→ 5c · Converge — fresh-eyes review

Every title, lower third and end card is rendered from the Video Component Kit with the job's real names — ProRes 4444 with alpha, identical in Premiere, Final Cut and Resolve. Never typed by hand in the app. /buwan:graphics

QA gate between build and converge · defects go back to rebuild, 3 rounds max

06 · Automatic

SFX

From placed graphics: risers into titles, a hit on the logo, swishes on lower thirds. Music bed flat −20 dB under dialogue. /buwan:sfx

07 · You

Review — taste only

Everything measurable already passed its gate. This pass is pacing feel and emotional build — wedding holds go to vows and first looks; corporate holds go to speakers and the keynote's key line. Which moments make the film is still the editor's call. /buwan:review

Clipper · second entry

Long-form → 9:16 reels/stories clips · re-enters the pipeline at 01. /buwan:clipper

Same-day note — a new wedding project gets the basics only (/buwan:basics). /buwan:sde is the full flow when the editor asks for it: Story-Lock → the music-driven cut → gates → title plate and end card → export. Gates still apply, rounds don't. Grade from a saved look, captions minimal, one converge pass.

Shared by every editor through the buwan Claude Code plugin (github.com/Buwan-Films/buwan-claude): Hailo-style footage prep (cull, sync, organize), Plotline-style story work (transcripts, speakers, focus, exclusions, Story-Lock), the studio's own rules, and your music.
