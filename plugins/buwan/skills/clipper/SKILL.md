# Clipper — long-form → reels

Reels re-enter the pipeline at 01: they get their own Story-Lock, gate and export, compressed.

1. **Pick moments** from the paper edit and transcript: lines that work alone in under 3 seconds of context — power statements, a vow line with a callback, a funny or tearful reaction, the kiss, the party peak. Propose 3–5 reels: `title · hook line (first 3s) · TC in/out · length (15–60s, max 90s) · music`.
2. **Hook**: the strongest line or image lands in the first second. Use kit 05.01 Hook screen or a burned-in caption, never a slow open.
3. **Reframe** to 1080×1920 (editor's app: Premiere Auto Reframe / Resolve Smart Reframe (Studio) / FCP Smart Conform), then check every shot by eye: faces inside the 1080×1350 safe zone, no cropped hands at the kiss/ring moments.
4. **Graphics**: 05.xx social components and burned-in captions (kit 07.06 style, ≤ 8 words per line), watermark crescent top-right (06.04 / social spec), 05.08 CTA end screen.
5. **Gate**: proof gate on the reel's EDL (fast cutting is expected; holds optional), audio: keep the house dialogue mix unless the owner sets a social loudness (common platform target is −14 LUFS, true peak −1 dBFS — ask before using it), grade inherits the film's.
6. **Export** `exports/<project>_reel-<n>_v1.mp4`, H.264 1080×1920, 23.976 or 30 fps per platform, AAC 320 kbps. Write a caption + 5 hashtags per reel for the editor to post (couple names only with the couple's permission).
Corporate: also a 1:1 1080×1080 LinkedIn cut of the recap.
